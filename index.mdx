FocusAlpha is a research operating system built for investment teams. It brings together every source of information you work with, lets you interrogate it through AI-powered chat, and surfaces the connections between companies and entities across your research.

## What you can do

**Ingest any document format.** Upload PDFs, Word documents, Excel files, emails, web clips, and meeting transcripts. FocusAlpha processes them all into a unified, searchable research library.

**Chat with your research library.** Ask questions across all your ingested documents. Responses include inline citations so you can trace every answer back to its source.

**Run the agent assistant.** The agent has tools to pull SEC filings and run stock screens, so you can go from a question to structured data without leaving FocusAlpha.

**Explore the knowledge graph.** FocusAlpha builds and maintains a graph of companies and entities extracted from your research, making it easy to trace relationships and spot connections across coverage.
