Taiwan Cash Flow provides the quarterly cash-flow statement for Taiwan-listed companies on TWSE and TPEx, plus the balance-sheet detail that [Taiwan Financials](/international/taiwan-financials) does not carry: cash, inventories, PP&E, borrowings, lease liabilities and net debt. Each observation is one company for one fiscal season, in TWD thousands. Coverage starts at 2024 Q1.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Taiwan Cash Flow For

Measure how much cash a Taiwan company generates and how much it reinvests. Build a quarterly capex and depreciation series for a semiconductor or hardware name. Track free cash flow, leverage and net cash. Check whether reported earnings are turning into cash.

## Why Cash Flow Is Its Own Endpoint

The summary tables behind [Taiwan Financials](/international/taiwan-financials) print **no cash-flow statement** and no balance-sheet detail beyond the three totals and share capital. The figures here are read from the inline XBRL originals of the same filed reports instead, so they carry a different set of fields and a shorter history.

If a cash, capex or borrowings question comes back empty from the financials endpoint, this is the endpoint to use.

## Cash Flows Are Filed Cumulatively

<Warning>
Like the income statement, TWSE cash-flow figures are **cumulative from the start of the fiscal year**. Season 2 is the first half and season 4 is the full year.

Reading a cumulative value as a quarter overstates the second half of the year.
</Warning>

Every flow line therefore comes back **twice**.

The `_ytd` fields — `operating_cf_ytd`, `investing_cf_ytd`, `financing_cf_ytd`, `fx_effect_ytd`, `net_change_ytd`, `capex_ytd`, `intangible_capex_ytd`, `depreciation_ytd`, `amortisation_ytd` and `free_cash_flow_ytd` — are the figure **as filed**, cumulative.

The `_q` fields with the same names are the **single quarter**.

## How to Read quarter_basis

`quarter_basis` says how the `_q` values were arrived at.

`as_reported` means season 1, where the cumulative figure is already the quarter.

`derived_from_ytd` means this season's cumulative figure minus the previous season's.

`null` means the previous season is missing, in which case the `_q` fields are null. **Those nulls must not be treated as zero.**

## Point-in-Time Fields Have No Quarter Version

`cash_begin`, `cash_end`, `cash`, `inventories`, `ppe`, `lt_borrowings`, `st_borrowings`, `lease_liab_current`, `lease_liab_noncurrent` and `net_debt` are balances at a date. They never accumulate, so they have no `_q` form.

## Key Taiwan Cash Flow Fields

`capex` is purchases of property, plant and equipment and is **negative**, as filed. `intangible_capex` is purchases of intangible assets, reported separately.

`free_cash_flow` is operating cash flow plus capex. Because capex is already negative, this is operating cash flow less capex, and it does not deduct `intangible_capex`.

`net_debt` is borrowings plus lease liabilities minus cash. **Negative means net cash.** A borrowing or lease field that is null on the row contributes nothing to `net_debt`, so check those fields before reading a small net debt as low leverage.

A null `financing_cf_ytd` is normal: a company with no financing activity in the period does not print the line.

## Check cf_closes Before Trusting a Row

`cf_closes` checks the filing against itself: the three subtotals plus the FX effect against the reported net change in cash.

`true` means the row adds up. `false` means it does not, and the row should not be trusted. `null` means one of the legs was missing, so it could not be checked.

## Consolidated Where the Company Files It

`basis` names the report the row came from: `cr` consolidated, `ir` parent-only, or `er`, the remaining variant. Consolidated is used where it exists. Companies with no subsidiaries file no consolidated statements, so their rows are `ir` rather than absent.

Do not mix bases when comparing companies.

## Taiwan Cash Flow Date Convention

`year` is the fiscal year and `season` is the TWSE season, 1 to 4. Each row also carries `period_start`, `period_end` and `period_months`, the length of the cumulative window: `3` for season 1 and `12` for season 4.

Rows are returned newest first, one row per company per season.

## Taiwan Cash Flow Units

Amounts are **TWD thousands**. The response states `currency` and `unit` explicitly — read them rather than assuming a scale.

## Taiwan Cash Flow Coverage

Coverage starts at **2024 Q1**, for about 2,700 companies. That is a shorter history than [Taiwan Financials](/international/taiwan-financials), which starts at 2013 Q1, because the inline XBRL originals are read directly.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/tw/cashflow

Quarterly cash-flow statements and balance-sheet detail, newest first.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A Taiwan security code such as `2330`, a suffixed ticker such as `2330.TW` or `6976.TWO`, an ISIN or a `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer">
  Fiscal year.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="season" type="integer">
  TWSE season, 1 to 4.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  `next_cursor` from the previous page.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="40">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000.
</ParamField>
