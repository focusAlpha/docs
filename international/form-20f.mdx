Form 20-F Data serves the content of a foreign private issuer's annual Form 20-F filing with the SEC. Each observation belongs to one filing, addressed by section: the filing profile, as-reported segments, disclosed ownership, the notes, and the filing's own XBRL facts. A 20-F is an **annual** filing — there are no quarterly rows in this dataset.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Form 20-F Data For

Read a non-US issuer's segment breakdown as it reported it. See the holders the filing disclosed. Pull a specific XBRL fact from the filing's own instance. Confirm which accounting standard and reporting currency a filing used before comparing it with anything.

## The Financial Statements Are Not in This Dataset

<Warning>
The three financial statements are **not** served here. [Income Statements](/financials/income-statements), [Balance Sheets](/financials/balance-sheets) and [Cash Flow Statements](/financials/cash-flow-statements) are the single contract for them, and a covered 20-F filer's annual IFRS statements are served there through the 20-F fallback.

Look for a 20-F filer's statements on the ordinary statement endpoints, not here.
</Warning>

## The Form 20-F Sections

`profile` says which filing this is: its date, its accession number, its reporting currency, and whether it reports under IFRS or US GAAP.

`segments` carries as-reported business and geographic breakdowns, filterable by `dimension`, `metric` or `fiscal_year`.

`ownership` carries the holders the filing disclosed.

`notes` carries the filing's note disclosures.

`xbrl-facts` carries the filing's own XBRL instance.

## Do Not Sum Form 20-F Ownership Rows

<Warning>
Board-as-a-group lines appear **beside** individual holders in the `ownership` section. Adding them together double-counts every board member.
</Warning>

## XBRL Facts Require a Narrowing Parameter

A single XBRL instance holds thousands of facts, so the `xbrl-facts` section **requires at least one** of `tag`, `accession` or `period_end`.

An unnarrowed request is rejected rather than returning an unusable page.

## Form 20-F Date Convention

A 20-F covers a **fiscal year**. `fiscal_year` on the segments section selects one. `period_end` on the XBRL section selects facts by their period end date.

## Form 20-F Sources

All content is read from the issuer's own Form 20-F filing on SEC EDGAR, including the XBRL instance the issuer filed with it.

## Form 20-F Coverage

Coverage is 321 foreign private issuers filing Form 20-F, from 2014-12, updated annually as each issuer files.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/20f/profile

Which filing this is: one object, not a list. It carries `ticker`, `name`, `country`, `form`, `filing_date`, `accession`, `filing_url`, the reporting `currency`, the `taxonomy` (`ifrs-full` or US GAAP), and `ownership_status` and `ownership_as_of` for the ownership section.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, CIK, ISIN or `company_id`.
</ParamField>

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/20f/notes

The notes to the accounts — accounting policies, risk disclosures, lease, pension and tax notes — in full text.

**Find the note first, then read it.** By default this returns the **index**: each note's readable `note_title` (for example `Leases`), its `note_tag` (the IFRS concept), `period_end` and `text_chars`, with no text. You do not have to know taxonomy concept names — list the index, pick by title, then set `include_text` on a narrowed request, and the text comes back in `content_text`. With `include_text` the page is capped at 10 rows, because a single note can run to 50,000 characters.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, CIK, ISIN or `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="title_contains" type="string">
  Substring of the note title, at least 2 characters, for example `lease`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="note_tag" type="string">
  The note's concept, with or without its taxonomy prefix — `DisclosureOfLeasesExplanatory` works as well as `ifrs-full:DisclosureOfLeasesExplanatory`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="accession" type="string">
  One filing's accession number, for example `0001628280-26-025362`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_text" type="boolean" default="false">
  Return the note text.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="200">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000 — capped at 10 when `include_text` is set. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/20f/segments

As-reported business and geographic breakdowns. Each row carries `dimension`, `segment_path`, `segment_name`, `depth`, `is_elimination`, `metric`, the XBRL `tag`, `period_end`, `fiscal_year`, `value` and `currency`.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, CIK, ISIN or `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="dimension" type="string">
  `geographic` or `business`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="metric" type="string">
  For example `revenue` or `assets`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="fiscal_year" type="integer">
  Fiscal year.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="200">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/20f/ownership

The holders the filing disclosed. Each row carries `holder_name`, `shares`, `ownership_pct`, `voting_pct`, `share_class`, `as_of` and `filing_url`. As noted above, a board-as-a-group line sits beside the individual holders — do not sum them.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, CIK, ISIN or `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="accession" type="string">
  One filing's accession number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="100">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/20f/xbrl-facts

The filing's own XBRL instance. Each row carries `taxonomy`, `tag`, `value`, `unit`, `decimals`, `period_start`, `period_end` and `is_instant`. At least one of `tag`, `accession` or `period_end` is required; a request with none returns `400`.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, CIK, ISIN or `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="tag" type="string">
  The concept name **without** its taxonomy prefix — `CashAndCashEquivalents`, not `ifrs-full:CashAndCashEquivalents`. The taxonomy comes back in its own `taxonomy` field.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="accession" type="string">
  One filing's accession number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="period_end" type="string">
  Period end date, `YYYY-MM-DD`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="200">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>
