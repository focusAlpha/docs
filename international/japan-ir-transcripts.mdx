Japan IR Transcripts are the records of what a Japanese company's management said, as the company published them on its own investor-relations website: results-briefing and conference-call transcripts, the speaker script published with a deck, and the Q&A record of briefings, small meetings and investor dialogues. Japanese companies rarely hold a US-style earnings call with a word-for-word transcript, so this is usually the only record of what was said and asked.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What you can use Japan IR transcripts for

Read what management told analysts after results, in the company's own published words. Find the questions investors asked at a segment small meeting. Compare this quarter's answers on a topic with last quarter's. Pair a transcript with the [deck](/international/japan-ir-presentation-decks) shown at the same briefing.

## Verbatim or the company's summary

<Warning>
Read `subtype` before quoting. `verbatim` is the words as spoken or scripted (transcripts, "Presentation Handout with Script", "Material with Speech Text"). `qa_summary` is the **company's own condensed account** of the questions and answers (要旨, "Main Q&A", "Q&A Summary") — about 70% of rows — and must be quoted as the company's summary, not as a speaker's words.
</Warning>

What a row is was decided from its **title**, not from the section of the website it was listed under — sites file Q&A transcripts under "earnings" and trade shows under "conference". `site_type` keeps the section the site used.

## Japan IR website coverage

Coverage is a fixed set of about 250 Japanese companies whose investor-relations websites are read continuously — not the whole market. `coverage.companies_covered` gives the current count; `coverage.available_from` and `available_to` give the date range for the company you asked about. History reaches the late 1990s where a site keeps an archive, and only recent years for most.

<Warning>
`company.in_ir_web_universe: false` means FocusAlpha does not read that company's investor-relations website. It never means the company published nothing. Many Japanese companies post only slides — try [Japan IR Presentation Decks](/international/japan-ir-presentation-decks).
</Warning>

The endpoint covers **Japanese companies only**: any other company returns `404`. If the website source cannot be read, the endpoint returns `503` rather than an empty list, so an empty `data` array always means the source answered and nothing matched.

## Key Japan IR transcript fields

- `title` and `title_language` (`en` or `ja`) — as the site wrote it, untranslated.
- `kind` is `transcript`; `subtype` is `verbatim` or `qa_summary`.
- `page_url` is the page the entry was found on; `media_urls` are the linked files.
- `documents` — each linked document's `format` (`pdf` or `html`), `url` and `chars`. `has_text` says whether any text was extracted.
- `ticker` — the listing the website is known by, which can be the ADR (Sony is `SONY`).

About 30% of website rows have no extracted text (`has_text: false`); the links are still served. That means "not extracted", not "empty".

The response also carries `company` (`company_id`, `ir_site_tickers`, `in_ir_web_universe`) and `counts`: `matched` for your filters, `in_this_tool` for all of this company's transcripts, `by_subtype`, `undated_excluded_by_date_filter` and `truncated_at_source`.

## Japan IR website date convention

Dates are as the website wrote them, and only a real calendar day becomes `date`. `date_status` says what the site gave:

| `date_status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `date` / `datetime` | A real day (`datetime` also carries a time, which is rare) |
| `period_label` | A fiscal period such as `2003-H1`, `2012-FY` or `1993-12`, kept in `period_label` and never turned into a day |
| `ambiguous` | A day/month-ambiguous numeric date such as `01/03/19`, not guessed |
| `invalid` / `missing` | No usable date |

`date_as_written` keeps the original. Rows are returned newest first: dated rows, then rows with only a `period_label`, then undated rows. `date_gte` and `date_lte` apply to `date` only; rows without one are dropped from a ranged answer and counted in `counts.undated_excluded_by_date_filter`.

## Japan IR transcript text

Pass `include_text=true` to add each document's extracted `text`, capped at `max_chars` per document, with `text_truncated`. Text extracted from PDFs loses its layout: quote it, but take reported figures from [Japan Financials](/international/japan-financials).

## GET /v1/companies/\{company\}/jp/ir-transcripts

<ParamField path="company" type="string" required>
  Tokyo symbol (`6758.T` or `6758`), five-digit EDINET security code, EDINET filer code, ADR symbol (`SONY`), ISIN or `company_id` of a Japanese company.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="subtype" type="string">
  `verbatim` or `qa_summary`. Any other value returns `400`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="q" type="string">
  Case-insensitive substring of the title, English or Japanese.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_gte" type="string">
  Earliest `date`, `YYYY-MM-DD`; `date_lte` sets the ceiling. Undated rows are excluded and counted.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_text" type="string" default="false">
  `true` adds each document's extracted text.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="max_chars" type="integer" default="20000">
  Text cap per document when `include_text=true`, between 1,000 and 200,000.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="50">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 200. Defaults to 10 when `include_text=true`. Page through with `cursor`, passing back `next_cursor` unchanged.
</ParamField>
