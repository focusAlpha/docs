Japan Financials provides the financial facts a Japanese-listed company tagged in its own filings: the annual securities report (有価証券報告書) and half-year report (半期報告書) filed to EDINET, the Financial Services Agency's filing system, and the earnings release (決算短信) filed to TDnet, the Tokyo Stock Exchange's timely-disclosure network. Each row is one tagged fact for one period, with a link to the filing it came from. Every response also carries a `metrics` block with derived ratios for the company's latest period.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What you can use Japan financials for

Read a Japanese company's accounts as it actually tagged them, including its five-year key-indicator table. Get a Japanese quarter, which since 2024 exists only in the TDnet earnings release. Trace any figure back to the original EDINET or TDnet document. Read the latest-period margins, returns and liquidity ratios without computing them yourself.

## These are the filer's own facts, not a normalised statement

<Warning>
There is **no `revenue` field**. Which element is revenue differs by accounting standard and by filer: Toyota tags `SalesRevenuesIFRS`, Sony `NetSalesIFRS`, a Japanese-GAAP industrial `NetSales`, a bank `OrdinaryIncomeBNK`.

**Match on `element_id`**, using the `element` parameter as a substring search. `label_ja` is the filer's own Japanese label and is not reliable to match on.
</Warning>

`element_id` keeps its namespace prefix, and the prefix tells you what you are holding:

| Prefix | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `jppfs_cor:` | Japanese-GAAP statements (consolidated or parent-only) |
| `jpigp_cor:` | IFRS statements |
| `jpcrp_cor:` | The annual report's own body: key indicators, cover page, headcounts, ratios |
| `tse-ed-t:` | TDnet earnings-release facts |

A company-specific prefix (for example `jpcrp030000-asr_E02144-000:`) marks an element the filer defined itself.

## Two sources, and `source` says which

- `edinet` — the statutory annual and half-year reports, plus quarterly reports filed before the quarterly report was abolished in 2024. EDINET has carried **no quarters since 2024**.
- `tdnet` — the company's own earnings release. This is the only place a recent Japanese quarter exists.

Both come back by default; pass `source` to keep one.

<Warning>
**TDnet values are cumulative from the start of the fiscal year**, not the quarter alone: a second-quarter release prints the first six months. `period_grain` says which (`3M`, `6M`, `9M` or `FY`) and is null on EDINET rows. Subtract two grains to get a single quarter, and never compare a `6M` figure with a `3M` one.
</Warning>

`report_period` maps to both sources: `annual` is the annual report or full-year release, `half` the half-year report or second-quarter release, and `quarterly` a pre-2024 quarterly report or a first- or third-quarter release.

## Consolidated, parent-only and `other`

`basis` follows the **taxonomy a fact was tagged in**, not the accounts it describes. Only the Japanese-GAAP statement taxonomy (`jppfs_cor:`) marks consolidated versus parent-only.

<Warning>
An IFRS filer tags its statements in `jpigp_cor:`, which carries no consolidation mark, so those rows come back with basis `other`. Filtering on `basis=consolidated` returns **nothing** for Sony's or Toyota's consolidated revenue. For an IFRS filer, read the `jpigp_cor:` elements, which are the consolidated accounts by construction; its `jppfs_cor:` rows are the parent-only ones.
</Warning>

On EDINET rows `basis` is returned as `Consolidated`, `NonConsolidated` or `Other`; on TDnet rows it is `consolidated` or `non_consolidated`. The filter parameter takes the lowercase form for both.

The key indicators (経営指標等, `jpcrp_cor:…SummaryOfBusinessResults`) appear twice per year under the same `element_id`: the bare context (`context_id` such as `CurrentYearDuration`) is the consolidated figure, and `…_NonConsolidatedMember` is the parent-only one. A `context_id` ending in a `…ReportableSegmentMember` is that segment's figure, not the group's. Read `context_id` before taking a value.

## Key Japan financial fields

- `value_num` holds a numeric fact and `value_text` anything untagged as a number; there is no combined `value` column.
- `unit` is the currency or unit of the row — usually `JPY`, but a filer can tag the same fact in more than one currency, so read it before comparing.
- `rel_period` is the filing's own word for which period a fact belongs to: `当期` (current), `前期` (prior), up to `四期前` (four years back). One annual report carries five years of key indicators, so one call gives history. A duration and an instant use different words — `当期` is the year, `当期末` its closing date — and `is_point_in_time` says which kind a row is. TDnet rows use English values such as `current`.
- `doc_id`, `doc_description`, `filed_on` and `filing_url` identify the source document. `accounting_standard` is set on TDnet rows.

Japanese-GAAP `ResearchAndDevelopmentExpensesSGA` is already inside SG&A — a breakdown line, not an addition to it.

## Amendments

An amended report (訂正報告書) restates figures. By default you get the **corrected** value; pass `include_superseded=true` to see the original as well.

## The metrics block

Every response carries a top-level `metrics` object with derived figures for the company's **latest** period: liquidity (`current_ratio`, `quick_ratio`, `cash_ratio`, `operating_cash_flow_ratio`), efficiency, leverage and coverage, margins, returns and year-on-year growth. These are the same numbers the [company screen](/screening/companies/by-fundamentals) filters and sorts on.

- `metrics.period` says which period it describes. It does **not** follow the `year`, `period_end` or `report_period` filters on the rows beside it.
- `metrics.amounts` are in whole units of `metrics.currency` (`unit: "ones"`), which is not always the scale of the statement rows.
- `metrics.ratios` are fractions (`0.2` = 20%), except `days_sales_outstanding` and `operating_cycle`, which are days.
- A null field means the input is not published for this market; `metrics: null` means FocusAlpha holds no fundamentals for the company at all.

## Japan financials coverage

About 3,800 Japanese-listed companies. Every response carries a measured `coverage` block: `available_from` and `available_to` are the earliest and latest filing loaded, `companies_covered` is how many companies have facts, and `companies_tracked` is the Japanese universe they are measured against. Older years are still being added, so read `coverage.available_from` rather than assuming a start year. `history_status` reads `all_tracked_filers_loaded`, `backfill_in_progress_majority_loaded` or `largest_cap_first_backfill_in_progress`; while a backfill is in progress, a missing smaller company means not yet loaded, not not filed.

There is **no forward guidance** here: EDINET filings carry no earnings forecast, and the forecast half of the earnings release is served by [Management Guidance](/events/guidance).

## GET /v1/companies/\{company\}/jp/financials

<ParamField path="company" type="string" required>
  Tokyo symbol (`6758.T` or `6758`), five-digit EDINET security code (`67580`), EDINET filer code (`E01777`), ADR symbol (`SONY`), ISIN or `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="source" type="string" default="all">
  `edinet`, `tdnet` or `all`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="report_period" type="string">
  `annual`, `half` or `quarterly`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="basis" type="string">
  `consolidated`, `non_consolidated` or `other`. `other` returns EDINET rows only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="element" type="string">
  Substring of `element_id`, e.g. `NetSales`, `OperatingIncome`, `Assets`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer">
  Calendar year of the period end, between 2000 and 2100.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="period_end" type="string">
  Exact period end, `YYYY-MM-DD`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_superseded" type="string" default="false">
  `true` also returns figures a later amendment restated.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="200">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000. Rows are ordered newest period first; page through with `cursor`, passing back `next_cursor` unchanged.
</ParamField>
