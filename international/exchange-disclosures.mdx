Exchange Disclosures is the filing index across six venues: the SEC (8-K and 6-K), Japan (TDnet), Korea (DART), Taiwan (TWSE and TPEx), Hong Kong (HKEXnews) and mainland China (cninfo). Each observation is one filing, newest first, in the language it was published in, with a link to the original document. One call covers every venue: omit `market` and it searches every venue the company files in.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

In each Asian market this is the authoritative local-market feed, and it includes disclosures that are often absent from SEC filings. It is also the only place filings arrive: the [news and events feed](/news/news-events) does not carry them.

## What You Can Use Exchange Disclosures For

Monitor what a company told its own market. Catch an earnings flash, a buyback progress report or an officer change hours before it reaches an English-language wire. Reach disclosures that never appear in an SEC filing at all.

<Note>
A foreign private issuer may also furnish a local announcement to the SEC on Form 6-K, and then it appears under both venues.
</Note>

## markets_searched Distinguishes Two Empty Results

`markets_searched` names the venues that were actually consulted for the company.

An empty result with an empty `markets_searched` means the company files in none of the six venues — a different fact from a company that files in one and announced nothing.

## Three Venues Are Archives, Three Are Weeks Old

<Warning>
Each venue starts on a different date:

| Venue | `market` | Starts |
|---|---|---|
| SEC 8-K and 6-K | `us` | 2025-01-02 |
| Mainland China | `china` | 2023-01-03 |
| Hong Kong | `hongkong` | 2026-01-01, English filings only |
| Japan | `japan` | 2026-06-15 |
| Taiwan | `taiwan` | 2026-07-22 |
| Korea | `korea` | 2026-07-28 |

An empty result for an earlier date is the venue's start, not a company that announced nothing. Per-venue start dates come back in the response's `coverage` block.

Do not build historical studies on the Japan, Taiwan and Korea feeds. Use the filings and financial statement datasets for history.
</Warning>

## What Each Venue Provides

**SEC** rows are the filing's **cover page**: `form`, `items`, `period_of_report`, `accepted_at`, `is_amendment`, and a title rendered from the item list (`8-K · Items 2.02, 9.01`), because an 8-K has no title. The exhibit text is in [8-K Exhibits](/events/8k-exhibits) and the extracted facts are in [Structured 8-K Events](/events/8k-events).

**Japan** links the document itself: `filing_url` is the PDF and `xbrl_url` carries the numbers for earnings releases. It also classifies every row in `category` — `earnings_flash`, `ma`, `buyback_progress`, `officer_change` and others.

**Korea** links the original DART document and names the `filer`, who is often **not the company**: a large-holdings report about Samsung Electronics is filed by Samsung C&T, and an insider report by the executive personally.

**Taiwan** carries the announcement's **full text**. `description_chars` says how long it is, and `include_text=true` returns it.

**Hong Kong** carries `released_at`, the minute the exchange released the document, HKEX's headline category in `category` with the finer `categories` and `category_codes`, and every stock code on a joint filing in `tickers`. Withdrawn announcements are left out here; [Hong Kong Disclosures](/international/hong-kong-disclosures) serves them.

**Mainland China** carries the filer's own numeric `announcement_types` and `has_clock_time`. A real clock exists only when `has_clock_time` is true. [Mainland China Disclosures](/international/china-disclosures) adds the type-code filter.

## Category Filtering Excludes Four Venues

Only Japan and Hong Kong classify their rows. `category` matches Japan's own classification or Hong Kong's headline category; filtering on it excludes the SEC, Korea, Taiwan and mainland-China rows entirely rather than returning them unfiltered.

## Exchange Disclosures Language

Titles and text are published in Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese and are **not translated**. Hong Kong indexes the English filing. A keyword search in English therefore finds only the SEC and Hong Kong rows.

## Exchange Disclosures Date Convention

`date_gte` and `date_lte` filter on the disclosure date in the local market. Rows are returned newest first across all searched venues.

## Exchange Disclosures Sources

Filings come from SEC EDGAR, TDnet, DART, TWSE and TPEx, HKEXnews and cninfo — the companies' own filings to their own markets.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/disclosures

Filings for one company across every venue it files in, newest first.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker (`AAPL`, `2330.TW`, `005930.KS`, `6758.T`, `0700.HK`, `600519.SS`), a `company_id`, a CIK or an ISIN.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="market" type="string">
  `us`, `japan`, `korea`, `taiwan`, `hongkong` or `china`. Omit to search every venue the company files in.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="category" type="string">
  Japan's own classification, for example `earnings_flash`, or Hong Kong's headline category. Filtering on it excludes the other four venues.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_gte" type="string">
  Earliest disclosure date, `YYYY-MM-DD`. `date_lte` sets the ceiling.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_text" type="boolean" default="false">
  Return the Taiwanese announcement's full text.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 200. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>
