Mainland China Report Timing answers one question: when did a Shanghai-, Shenzhen- or Beijing-listed company's periodic report first become public? Each observation is one company and one period end, newest first, from 2023 onward, carrying the timestamp of the **earliest** announcement that published the report.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use China Report Timing For

Build a backtest that only sees A-share numbers after the market did. Measure how long after period end a company reports. Pair each row of [Mainland China Financials](/international/china-financials) with the moment it became public.

## Why the Date on a Statement Row Is Not Enough

<Warning>
For Asian markets, the date that travels with standardized statement data is routinely the **period end**, or the date of a later filing that repeated the period as a comparative — not the publication date.

A backtest built on that date sees the numbers months before the market did. Use `first_announced_at` from this endpoint, or `disclosed_at` on the financials row, which carries the same timestamp.
</Warning>

## The Earliest Filing Wins

A periodic report is filed as several announcements — the main text, the summary and sometimes an English version. `first_announced_at` is the earliest of them, which is the moment the numbers became public. `doc_count` says how many announcements carried the report, and `announcement_id` names the one the timestamp came from, so you can look it up in [Mainland China Disclosures](/international/china-disclosures).

## A Midnight Timestamp Is Not a Time

<Warning>
When `has_clock_time` is false, the index gave a date and no clock — it does this for anything filed on a non-trading day. `first_announced_at` is then midnight Beijing time, which reads as `16:00Z` on the previous day in UTC. That is the absence of a time, not an announcement at 4 p.m.

`announced_on` is the Beijing calendar day and is always safe to use.
</Warning>

## Key China Report Timing Fields

Each row carries `sec_code`, `company_id`, `sec_name`, `period_end`, `first_announced_at`, `announced_on`, `has_clock_time`, `doc_count` and `announcement_id`.

## China Report Timing Coverage

Coverage runs from 2023-01 onward, the start of the announcement archive, and updates daily.

## China Report Timing Sources

Timestamps are read from cninfo, the disclosure site the Chinese securities regulator designates.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/cn/report-timing

When each periodic report of one company first became public, newest period first.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A six-digit security code (`600519`), a suffixed ticker (`600519.SS`, `000333.SZ`, `920680.BJ`), an ISIN or a `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer">
  Calendar year of the period end.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="40">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 200.
</ParamField>
