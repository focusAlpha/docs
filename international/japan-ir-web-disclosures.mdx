Japan IR Website Disclosures is everything a Japanese company lists on its own investor-relations website that is not a transcript or a slide deck: news releases, dividend announcements, forecast revisions, links to its statutory filings, and named kinds such as the earnings release (決算短信) as posted on the company site — often in English, which the exchange feed does not carry — and AGM notices and resolutions.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What you can use Japan IR website disclosures for

Read a Japanese earnings release in English when the company publishes one. Get the AGM notice with the agenda and director slate, and the notice of what the meeting resolved. See when a company says it will report results or hold a briefing. Follow press releases and dividend announcements from the company's own site.

## Subtypes

Named kinds:

| `subtype` | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `results_summary` | The earnings release (決算短信) as posted on the company site, often in English |
| `agm_convocation` | The AGM notice (招集ご通知) with the agenda and director slate |
| `agm_resolution` | The notice of resolutions (決議ご通知) |
| `agm_other` | Other AGM material: Q&A pages, electronic-provision links, responses to proxy advisers |
| `schedule_notice` | The company announcing when it will report results or hold a briefing |
| `webcast_link` | A results call or webcast entry with no document posted behind it |
| `company_event` | Trade shows and product seminars |

Any other `subtype` is the **website's own section** passed through as found — for example `press_release`, `earnings`, `filing`, `dividend` or `other`. `counts.by_subtype` lists the ones this company has, so you can see what exists before filtering.

What a row is was decided from its **title**, not from the section of the website it was listed under; `site_type` keeps the section the site used. Transcripts and slides have their own endpoints: [Japan IR Transcripts](/international/japan-ir-transcripts) and [Japan IR Presentation Decks](/international/japan-ir-presentation-decks).

## Japan IR website coverage

Coverage is a fixed set of about 250 Japanese companies whose investor-relations websites are read continuously — not the whole market. `coverage.companies_covered` gives the current count; `coverage.available_from` and `available_to` give the date range for the company you asked about. History reaches the late 1990s where a site keeps an archive, and only recent years for most.

<Warning>
`company.in_ir_web_universe: false` means FocusAlpha does not read that company's investor-relations website. It never means the company published nothing.
</Warning>

The endpoint covers **Japanese companies only**: any other company returns `404`. If the website source cannot be read, the endpoint returns `503` rather than an empty list.

## Key Japan IR website disclosure fields

- `title` and `title_language` (`en` or `ja`) — as the site wrote it, untranslated.
- `kind` is `ir_web_disclosure`; `subtype` is a named kind or the site's section.
- `page_url` is the page the entry was found on; `media_urls` are the linked files.
- `documents` — each linked document's `format` (`pdf` or `html`), `url` and `chars`. `has_text` says whether any text was extracted; about 30% of website rows have none, which means "not extracted", not "empty".

The response also carries `company` (`company_id`, `ir_site_tickers`, `in_ir_web_universe`) and `counts`: `matched`, `in_this_tool`, `by_subtype`, `undated_excluded_by_date_filter` and `truncated_at_source`.

## Japan IR website date convention

Only a real calendar day becomes `date`; `date_status` is `date`, `datetime`, `period_label` (a fiscal period such as `2012-FY`, never turned into a day), `ambiguous` (such as `01/03/19`, not guessed), `invalid` or `missing`, and `date_as_written` keeps the original. Rows come newest first — dated rows, then period-labelled rows, then undated rows. `date_gte` and `date_lte` apply to `date` only, and rows without one are counted in `counts.undated_excluded_by_date_filter`. See [Japan IR Transcripts](/international/japan-ir-transcripts#japan-ir-website-date-convention) for the full table.

Pass `include_text=true` to add each document's extracted `text`, capped at `max_chars`, with `text_truncated`. Take reported figures from [Japan Financials](/international/japan-financials), which reads the tagged values.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company\}/jp/ir-web-disclosures

<ParamField path="company" type="string" required>
  Tokyo symbol (`6758.T` or `6758`), five-digit EDINET security code, EDINET filer code, ADR symbol (`SONY`), ISIN or `company_id` of a Japanese company.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="subtype" type="string">
  A named kind (`results_summary`, `agm_convocation`, `schedule_notice`, …) or the site's own section (`press_release`, `dividend`, …).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="q" type="string">
  Case-insensitive substring of the title, English or Japanese.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_gte" type="string">
  Earliest `date`, `YYYY-MM-DD`; `date_lte` sets the ceiling. Undated rows are excluded and counted.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_text" type="string" default="false">
  `true` adds each document's extracted text.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="max_chars" type="integer" default="20000">
  Text cap per document when `include_text=true`, between 1,000 and 200,000.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="50">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 200. Defaults to 10 when `include_text=true`. Page through with `cursor`, passing back `next_cursor` unchanged.
</ParamField>
