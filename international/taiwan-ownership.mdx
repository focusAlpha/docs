Taiwan Ownership covers who owns a Taiwan-listed company from the inside. The `directors` kind is the monthly filing of shares held by directors, supervisors, managers and major shareholders, including the shares they have **pledged** as loan collateral. The `major_shareholders` kind is the exchange's list of large holders, by name. Both are share counts, not money.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Taiwan Ownership For

Track what a Taiwan company's chairman, directors and officers hold month by month. Find insiders who have pledged a large share of their holding, a governance signal a price chart does not show. See who the exchange lists as a company's major shareholders.

This is domestic insider ownership. For how much of a company foreign investors hold, use [Taiwan Foreign Ownership](/international/taiwan-foreign-ownership), which answers a different question.

## Directors' and Officers' Holdings

`kind=directors` is the default. Each row is one person in one role for one month.

`title` is the role as filed, in Chinese: for example 董事長本人 (the chairman), 董事本人 (a director), 獨立董事本人 (an independent director), 副總經理本人 (a vice president), 大股東本人 (a major shareholder), and 董事之法人代表人 (the representative of a corporate director).

`current_shares` is the holding. `elected_shares` is the holding at election, and is `0` for officers who were not elected. `pledged_shares` is the number pledged, and `pledged_pct` is pledged shares as a percentage of `current_shares`, on a **0-to-100 scale**.

## One Person Can Appear Under Several Titles

<Warning>
A person who holds two roles is listed once per role, **with the same holding on each row**. In the example below, TSMC's chairman appears as both 董事長本人 and 總經理本人 with the same `current_shares`.

Deduplicate by `holder_name` before adding up insider holdings, or the same shares are counted twice.
</Warning>

## The Major-Shareholder List Carries No Holding Size

<Warning>
`kind=major_shareholders` returns **names only**. The source publishes no share count, no percentage and no rank.

The row order is not a ranking, and there is nothing in these rows to sum.
</Warning>

The list is a snapshot taken on most days, so the same name repeats once per `snapshot_date`. Take the newest `snapshot_date` rather than reading the repeats as separate holdings.

Not every company has rows in the list. TSMC (`2330`), for example, returns none.

## Taiwan Ownership Date Convention

Directors' holdings are monthly. `period` is the month, `YYYY-MM`, and `report_date` is when it was filed. `date_gte` and `date_lte` take `YYYY-MM-DD`, but only the month is compared, so any day in a month selects that month.

Major-shareholder rows are dated by `snapshot_date`, `YYYY-MM-DD`, and `date_gte` and `date_lte` compare the full date.

Rows are returned newest first.

## Page Through One Month at a Time

Pass `next_cursor` back as `cursor` to get the next page. A person listed under two titles in the same month can straddle a page boundary, so fetch one month per request with `date_gte`, `date_lte` and a `limit` large enough to hold it.

## Taiwan Ownership Coverage

Directors' holdings run monthly from **2026-06**. The major-shareholder list runs from **2026-07-23**. Both are recent additions, so there is no longer history to backfill.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/tw/ownership

Directors' and officers' holdings, or the major-shareholder list, newest first.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A Taiwan security code such as `2330`, a suffixed ticker such as `2330.TW` or `6976.TWO`, an ISIN or a `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="kind" type="string" default="directors">
  `directors` or `major_shareholders`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="holder" type="string">
  Part of the holder's name. Matches `holder_name` for directors and `shareholder_name` for major shareholders.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_gte" type="string">
  Earliest date, `YYYY-MM-DD`. For directors, only the month is compared.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_lte" type="string">
  Latest date, `YYYY-MM-DD`. For directors, only the month is compared.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  `next_cursor` from the previous page.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="100">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000.
</ParamField>
