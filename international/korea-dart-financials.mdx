Korea DART Financials provides financial facts for a Korean-listed company as filed to DART, the Korean regulator's disclosure system. Each observation is one account line for one reporting period, in KRW, carrying the filer's own account concept and label. Coverage runs from 2022 onward.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Korea DART Financials For

Read a Korean company's accounts as it actually files them. Build a series for a specific account concept across years. Compare consolidated against parent-only figures. Trace any figure back to the original DART document.

## These Are the Filer's Own Accounts, Not a Normalised Statement

<Warning>
There is **no `revenue` field** on the rows. Which Korean account is revenue differs by filer and by industry, so the rows do not pick one.

Each row carries `account_id`, the machine concept — for example `ifrs-full_CostOfSales` or `dart_OperatingIncomeLoss` — and `account_nm`, the filer's own label in Korean.

**Match on `account_id`.** Matching Korean label text is unreliable.
</Warning>

## The Korean Reporting Calendar Has No Q2

`report_period` is `annual`, `half`, `q1` or `q3`. The Korean reporting year has a **half-year report and no standalone Q2**. A pipeline that expects four quarterly reports will mis-read the middle of the year.

## An Interim Row Does Not Always Cover the Period Its Name Suggests

<Warning>
DART prints both a three-month and a year-to-date column in interim reports, the filer decides which to fill, and only the first is served. The two statements behave differently:

- **Income figures** in a `half` or `q3` row are the **single quarter** for most filers, but year-to-date for some. To check, divide the half-year figure by the `q1` figure: about 1 means the quarter alone, about 2 means year-to-date. Or reconcile against the annual report.
- **Cash-flow figures** in interim rows are **year-to-date**: the half-year cash flow covers six months and the `q3` cash flow covers nine.

Do not assume a `half` row covers six months, and do not assume it covers three.
</Warning>

## Most Filers Print Only the Combined Income Statement

Most Korean filers print a single statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income rather than a separate income statement. For them, `statement=income` returns nothing, and the income lines are under `statement=comprehensive_income`. If `income` comes back empty, query `comprehensive_income` before concluding the data is missing.

Two definitions also vary by filer: SG&A may or may not include R&D, and `FinanceCosts` is not the same as interest expense.

## Consolidated and Separate Are Different Books

`basis` is `consolidated` or `separate`, meaning parent-only. Both are filed. Never mix them in one comparison.

## Key Korea DART Financial Fields

`statement` selects one of `balance_sheet`, `income`, `comprehensive_income`, `cash_flow` or `changes_in_equity`.

Each row carries the amount in the row's `currency`, which is KRW, plus **the two prior periods as filed** in `amount_prior_period` and `amount_two_periods_prior`.

Every row carries `filing_url`, the original DART document behind the figure. Cite that when quoting a number.

## The metrics Block

Next to the rows, the response carries a `metrics` object: derived figures for the company's **latest** period, the same values [Company Screening](/screening/companies/by-fundamentals) filters and sorts on.

- `period`, `grain` and `filed_at` say which period the block describes. It does **not** follow the `year` or `report_period` you asked for.
- `amounts` — such as `revenue`, `net_income`, `free_cash_flow` and `cash_and_equivalents` — are in whole units of `currency`, with `unit` of `ones`.
- `ratios` are **fractions**, so 0.2 means 20%, except `days_sales_outstanding` and `operating_cycle`, which are days.
- `growth` holds year-on-year growth rates, also as fractions, and `flags` holds `is_lossmaking` and `equity_negative`.

A null field in the block means the source does not publish what it needs, not zero. `metrics` itself is null when no fundamentals are held for the company.

## Korea DART Financials Coverage

<Warning>
Coverage is 2022 onward and is still backfilling, newest year and largest cap first.

A missing older year for a small company means **not yet loaded**, not not filed.
</Warning>

## Korea DART Financials Date Convention

`year` is the business year and is **required**; omitting it returns `400`. `report_period` names which of the year's reports the row came from.

## Korea DART Financials Sources

Facts are filed by the companies to DART. Account concepts and labels are the filer's own. Nothing is normalised, translated or restated.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/kr/financials

Account-level financial facts for one business year. The route accepts a Korean corp code, a suffixed ticker such as `005930.KS`, an ISIN or a `company_id` directly.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A Korean DART corp code (8 digits), a suffixed ticker, an ISIN or a `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer" required>
  Business year.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="report_period" type="string">
  `annual`, `half`, `q1` or `q3`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="basis" type="string">
  `consolidated` or `separate`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="statement" type="string">
  `balance_sheet`, `income`, `comprehensive_income`, `cash_flow` or `changes_in_equity`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  `next_cursor` from the previous page.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="200">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000.
</ParamField>
