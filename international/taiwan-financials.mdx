Taiwan Financials provides quarterly financial statements for Taiwan-listed companies on TWSE and TPEx. Each observation is one statement for one fiscal season, in TWD thousands, as filed with the Taiwan exchanges. Coverage starts at 2013 Q1.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Taiwan Financials For

Build a quarterly revenue and margin history for a Taiwan-listed company. Compare a quarter against the same quarter a year earlier. Pair the quarterly statements with [Taiwan Monthly Revenue](/international/taiwan-monthly-revenue), which reports far sooner.

## Taiwan Income Statements Are Filed Cumulatively

<Warning>
Read this before using any income figure. TWSE files income-statement values **cumulative from the start of the fiscal year**. Season 2 is the first half and season 4 is the full year.

Reading a cumulative value as a quarter overstates Q4 by roughly the whole year.
</Warning>

Because of that, every income line comes back **twice**.

The `_ytd` fields — `revenue_ytd` and its siblings — are the figure **as filed**, cumulative.

The `_q` fields — `revenue_q` and its siblings — are the **single quarter**.

## How to Read quarter_basis

`quarter_basis` says how the `_q` value was arrived at.

`as_reported` means season 1, where the cumulative figure is already the quarter and no arithmetic was applied.

`derived_from_ytd` means this season's cumulative figure minus the previous season's.

`null` means the previous season is missing, in which case the `_q` fields are null. **Those nulls must not be treated as zero.**

## Income Rows and Balance Rows Are Different Shapes

`statement` is `income` or `balance`. There is **no cash-flow statement** in this dataset: the summary tables it is built from print none, and no balance-sheet detail beyond total assets, total liabilities, total equity and share capital. Cash, inventories, PP&E, borrowings, net debt, capex, depreciation and free cash flow come from [Taiwan Cash Flow](/international/taiwan-cash-flow) instead. An empty answer here to a cash question means the wrong endpoint, not missing data.

Balance-sheet rows are point-in-time: never cumulative and never derived. Their income columns are null, and income rows have null balance columns.

## Line Items Come Back Only When You Ask

By default the rows carry the statement **totals**. Pass `line_items=true` to add the detail the filing also prints: cost of revenue, operating expenses, the tax line, the current and non-current split, the equity components, and the loan and deposit lines that banks file instead of a classified balance sheet. Most of these are null for any one industry.

An empty cost of revenue without `line_items=true` is the flag, not missing data.

Two line items need care:

- `gross_profit` is struck **after** the intercompany elimination lines. Revenue less cost of revenue reaches `gross_profit_before_elimination`.
- Banks and financial holdings filed the income tax line with the **opposite sign** before 2018. Read `income_tax_basis` rather than subtracting the tax line blindly.

## Taiwan Financials Date Convention

`year` is the fiscal year and `season` is the TWSE season, 1 to 4. A season is a fiscal quarter, but because income is filed cumulatively, the season number describes the **end** of the cumulative window on the `_ytd` fields and the quarter itself on the `_q` fields.

## Taiwan Financials Units

Amounts are **TWD thousands**. The response states `currency` and `unit` explicitly — read them rather than assuming a scale.

<Warning>
The per-share fields — `eps_basic_ytd`, `eps_basic_q` and `book_value_per_share` — are in **plain TWD per share**, not thousands. TSMC's `eps_basic_ytd` of `49.33` is NT$49.33. Applying `unit` to every number overstates EPS a thousandfold.
</Warning>

## The metrics Block

Next to the rows, the response carries a `metrics` object: derived figures for the company's **latest** period, the same values [Company Screening](/screening/companies/by-fundamentals) filters and sorts on.

- `period` and `grain` say which period the block describes. It does **not** follow the `year` or `season` you asked for.
- `amounts` are in **whole TWD**, with `unit` of `ones` — a different scale from the rows beside them, which are in thousands.
- `ratios` are **fractions**, so 0.2 means 20%, except `days_sales_outstanding` and `operating_cycle`, which are days.
- `growth` holds year-on-year growth rates, also as fractions, and `flags` holds `is_lossmaking` and `equity_negative`.

A null field in the block means the source does not publish what it needs, not zero: Taiwan publishes no receivables balance and no interest expense, so the fields built on them are null for every Taiwan company. `metrics` itself is null when no fundamentals are held for the company.

## Taiwan Financials Sources

Statements are filed by the companies with the Taiwan exchanges. The `_ytd` values are as filed. The `_q` values are computed by FocusAlpha except in season 1, and `quarter_basis` says which case each row is.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/tw/financials

Quarterly income statements and balance sheets, newest first. The route accepts a Taiwan security code such as `2330`, a suffixed ticker such as `2330.TW`, an ISIN or a `company_id` directly — there is no need to resolve the identifier first.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A Taiwan security code, a suffixed ticker, an ISIN or a `company_id`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="statement" type="string">
  `income` or `balance`. Both by default.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer">
  Fiscal year.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="season" type="integer">
  TWSE season, 1 to 4.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="line_items" type="boolean" default="false">
  `true` adds the line-item detail described above.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  `next_cursor` from the previous page.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="40">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000.
</ParamField>
