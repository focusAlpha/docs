Segment Breakouts are the segment, product-line and geography figures a company printed in its earnings release — the rows that sit **beneath** the consolidated figure. Each observation is one figure for one segment from one release, carrying the resolved `segment_name`, the cut it belongs to, whether it may be added, and the verbatim quote it was read from.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Segment Breakouts For

See how a quarter split across a company's reportable segments, product lines or regions, the day it was announced. Track segment KPIs the financial statements never carry. Compare the as-printed split with the audited segment detail.

## Split on segment_axis Before Summing

<Warning>
One release routinely prints **several cuts of the same revenue**. Apple prints Products and Services, iPhone and Mac, and Americas, Europe and Greater China — and each cut adds up to the total on its own. Summing a mixed page counts the quarter more than once.

Group by `segment_axis` first. Then use `segment_role` to decide what may be added:

| `segment_role` | May you add it? |
|---|---|
| `unit` | Yes |
| `reconciling` | Yes, **with its sign** — Corporate and Other, Eliminations |
| `subtotal` | No — it is already the sum of other rows |

An empty axis is its own group and must never be added into a known one.
</Warning>

## Read segment_name

A release often prints the segment name only in the row label and leaves the segment field empty. `segment_name` resolves it. `segment_as_extracted` keeps the field as extracted and `label_qualifier` the part of the label the name came from.

## Not Every Breakout Is Here, on Purpose

A row counts as a segment only when the release named the segment in its own field. When the name appears only inside the row label, the row cannot be told apart from a wording variant, so it is left out rather than guessed at.

Cisco's Q2 FY26 geographies are the worked case: they are not here. They are reachable through [Reported Earnings Figures](/events/earnings-results) with no `level`.

## Key Segment Breakout Fields

Each row carries the same release and value fields as [Company Measures](/events/8k-company-measures) — `ticker`, `company_id`, `year`, `quarter`, `observed_at`, `doc_id`, `source_ref`, `metric_key`, `basis`, `period`, `period_end`, `label`, `value_text`, `value_low`, `value_high`, `value_unit`, `yoy`, `quote`, `value_is_change` and `unit_check` — plus `segment_name`, `segment_as_extracted`, `label_qualifier`, `segment_axis` and `segment_role`.

Read `value_is_change` before using a number: when true, the figure is a change, not a level.

## Segment Breakouts Coverage

Companies whose releases break a line out and name the segment in its own field. The floor moves as the backfill proceeds — read `coverage.available_from` on the response.

## Segment Breakouts Date Convention

`year` and `quarter` are the fiscal year and quarter of the **release**. `period` is the period the figure is **for**, as the release labelled it. Rows are returned newest release first.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/8k-segments

Segment, product and geography breakouts from one company's earnings releases, newest first. The identifier is expanded to every ticker the company is known by.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, `company_id`, CIK or ISIN.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="metric_key" type="string">
  Normalised metric, for example `revenue` or `operating_income`. The pre-normalisation spelling is accepted too.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="period" type="string">
  The period the figure is for, exactly as the release labelled it, for example `Q3-FY26`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer">
  Fiscal year of the release.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="quarter" type="integer">
  Fiscal quarter of the release, 1 to 4.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="100">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 1000. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>

## Related Datasets

See also [Company Measures](/events/8k-company-measures) for the consolidated figures these break down, [Reported Earnings Figures](/events/earnings-results) for every row the release contained, and [Segmented Financials](/financials/segments) for the segment detail in the audited statements — a different, restated record.
