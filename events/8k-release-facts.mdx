Release Facts are every number a company printed in its earnings release: each cell of each table and each figure in the text. Each row is one figure, carrying where it was printed — the table title, the row label and the rows above it, the column header, or the sentence — together with what it is: the metric, the segment, the basis and the period.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call
</Info>

## What You Can Use Release Facts For

Read the parts of a release the headline figures leave out: the segment and product-line tables, the KPI pages (units, prices, store counts, same-store sales, backlog, bookings, subscribers), the non-GAAP reconciliation line by line, the statements and cash flow as printed, and the guidance tables. [Reported Earnings Figures](/events/earnings-results) keeps the handful of figures a reader looks at first; this dataset is the whole document.

## Start With the Table of Contents

Call the route with only the company and you get the release's table of contents: every table with its title and type, the segments it names, the most-printed metrics and the periods it covers. Then ask for figures:

| Parameter | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `q` | Words that must all appear in the row label, metric, segment, table title or sentence — `data center`, `backlog`, `same-store sales` |
| `table` (+ `exhibit`) | One table, by the number the table of contents lists |
| `segment`, `level` | One segment, or only the company's own figures, or only the breakouts |
| `current=true` | The quarter the release reports, with its year-to-date, full year and closing balance sheet |
| `releases=all` | Every release held for the company — a series across quarters |

## One Row Per Figure

A number printed in the summary table, the income statement and a sentence comes back **once**, from its finest printing — a table cell before a rounded sentence — and `mentions` says how many places printed it. Pass `evidence=true` for every printing.

<Warning>
Two **different** values under one metric are both returned and never merged. They are different lines — `Net income` beside `Net income -- TTM` in one column, `Average assets` beside `Total assets` — so read `row_label`, `row_path`, `col_header` and `table_title` before treating two rows as the same figure.
</Warning>

## Read These Before Using a Number

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `value`, `value_text` | `value` is scaled to whole units of `unit` / `currency`; `value_text` is exactly as printed |
| `is_change` | `true` means a change — growth, "up $50 million" — not a level |
| `is_forward` | `true` means guidance or outlook |
| `basis` | Empty is the as-reported (GAAP) figure; otherwise `non_gaap`, `adjusted`, `organic`, `constant_currency` … |
| `scope` | `partial` means only part of the company — a joint-venture share, one side of a split |
| `segment_axis` | `segment`, `product`, `geography` …; sum segments only within one axis |
| `period`, `period_end` | `period` is the company's own fiscal label; `period_end` the calendar day it ends, with `period_end_source` saying whether the release printed it or it came from the company's filed fiscal calendar |

## As Printed on the Day, and Restated Beside It

`value` is the figure the release printed that day. When the company later gave the same quarter another figure, both versions are kept: the restated one sits beside the printed one, with where it came from.

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `restated_value` | The figure the company later gave for the same quarter |
| `restated_basis` | `restated_in_filing` (a later 10-Q or 10-K replaced the first figure), `revised_after_release` (the first 10-Q or 10-K already differed from the release), or `fiscal_calendar_changed` (the company moved its fiscal year; the filed period is a transition stub, not the same quarter) |
| `restated_in_accession`, `restated_filed` | The filing that carries the restated figure, and the day it was filed |

Quote `value` for what the market saw on the day; use the restated figure or the [Financial Statements](/financials/combined) to compare across periods. The restated version is attached to the headline figures checked against the company's filings — revenue, operating income, net income, gross profit and diluted EPS.

## Release Facts Coverage

Most S&P 500 companies and, for nearly all of them, every quarterly earnings release filed since January 2025 — seven quarters by October 2026. The layer is not yet updated daily: a release filed after `coverage.available_to` has not been read yet, so the newest quarter can be missing here while [Reported Earnings Figures](/events/earnings-results) already has its headline figures. Releases filed only as PDF are not read. Read the `coverage` block on the response for the current count and date range. For other companies and earlier quarters, [Reported Earnings Figures](/events/earnings-results) holds the headline figures of far more releases.

## GET /v1/companies/\{company_id\}/8k-release-facts

The table of contents of a company's newest release, or its figures, depending on the parameters.

<ParamField path="company_id" type="string" required>
  A ticker, `company_id`, CIK or ISIN.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="view" type="string">
  `index` (table of contents) or `facts`. Omitted: no filter returns `index`, any filter returns `facts`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="q" type="string">
  Words that must all appear in the row label, metric, segment, table title or sentence.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="metric_key" type="string">
  A metric key from the table of contents, for example `revenue` or `operating_income`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="segment" type="string">
  Part of a segment or sub-segment name, for example `Data Center`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="level" type="string">
  `company` for figures with no segment, `segment` for the breakouts.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="basis" type="string">
  `gaap` (as reported) or `non_gaap` (any non-GAAP or adjusted basis).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="period" type="string">
  The fiscal label the figure is for: `Q2-FY26`, `H1-FY26`, `9M-FY26`, `FY26`, `AS_OF`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="current" type="boolean">
  Only the quarter the release reports, with its year-to-date, full year and closing balances.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="table" type="integer">
  One table, by the number the table of contents lists. Pass `exhibit` too when the release has more than one.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="source" type="string">
  `table` for table cells only, `prose` for figures from the text only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="accession" type="string">
  One release by accession number. Default: the newest.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="releases" type="string" default="latest">
  `all` searches every release held for the company.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="evidence" type="boolean" default="false">
  Every printing of every figure instead of one row per figure.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="100">
  Rows per page, between 1 and 500. Page through with `cursor`.
</ParamField>
