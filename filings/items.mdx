SEC Filing Items splits a single SEC filing into the numbered items the form defines, each with its text. Each observation is one item of one filing — Item 1A risk factors, Item 7 management's discussion and analysis, an 8-K event item, or a section of an IPO prospectus. This is the endpoint for reading a named section of a named filing, as opposed to searching across filings by meaning.

<Info>
  **Plan:** Free and above · **Credits:** 1 per call · **MCP tool:** `get_filing_items`
</Info>

## What You Can Use SEC Filing Items For

Read a company's risk factors or MD&A in full. Diff one year's Item 1A against the previous year's. Pull the exact 8-K item text behind a structured event. Read an IPO prospectus's use of proceeds, dilution or underwriting section. Feed a specific section into a model rather than the whole filing.

## SEC Filing Items Coverage

The form types supported are `10-K`, `10-Q` and `8-K`, split from EDGAR on request, and registration statements — `S-1`, `F-1`, `S-11`, their amendments, released `DRS` drafts and final `424B4` / `424B1` prospectuses — filed from January 2026, stored daily with their sections. The [item types endpoint](/filings/coverage) lists which item codes exist for each 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K form type.

## How to Address One Filing

A filing is addressed by ticker, form type and year, plus one more field depending on the form.

For a **10-K**, ticker, `filing_type=10-K` and `year` are enough.

For a **10-Q**, also pass `quarter` as 1 to 4.

For an **8-K**, also pass the `accession_number`, because a company files many 8-Ks in a year. Get it from the [SEC Filings Index](/filings/index).

`year` is the calendar year of the filing's **period of report**, not the year it was filed.

## Read an IPO prospectus

Pass `filing_type=S-1` to read a company's newest registration document, whichever form it is: S-1, F-1 or S-11, an amendment, a released draft or the final prospectus. `F-1`, `S-11`, `424B4` or `DRS` narrow it to that form, and `accession_number` picks one filing. No `year` is needed.

Address the company by `ticker`, the proposed ticker, or `cik`. A company that has not listed has no ticker, so take the CIK from [IPO Filings](/filings/ipo-filings), which also lists every filing's accession number.

A prospectus's items are section names: `cover` (offering terms and underwriters), `summary`, `the_offering`, `summary_financial_data`, `risk_factors`, `forward_looking`, `market_data`, `use_of_proceeds`, `dividend_policy`, `capitalization`, `dilution`, `mdna`, `business`, `management`, `executive_compensation`, `related_party`, `principal_holders`, `selling_holders`, `description_of_securities`, `shares_eligible`, `tax`, `underwriting`, `legal_matters`, `experts`, `where_to_find` and `financial_statements`. The 10-K codes `Item-1`, `Item-1A`, `Item-7`, `Item-8` and `Item-10` to `Item-13` name the same sections, so one `item` works on both forms. `item=all` returns the whole document.

<Note>
A prospectus runs to a million characters. Asked for with no `item`, it returns only its summary plus `table_of_contents`, which lists every section and its length. Ask for the sections you need by name. Through the MCP tool, a section too long for one result is cut and names the `text_offset` to read on from.
</Note>

A prospectus response also carries `form`, `public_date` (the day it became public on EDGAR), `offering_kind`, `primary_doc_url` and `index_url`. An S-1 is not necessarily an IPO: `offering_kind` says whether this one is.

### Prospectus tables

Two options are for programs that parse a prospectus's tables. They are on the REST endpoint only, not the MCP tool:

- `text_format=sec-html-1` returns the stored table-preserving text — one table row per line, cells separated by ` | ` — instead of the body. A format not stored for the filing returns `404`.
- `include_tables=true` adds `tables`: every figure in the prospectus's tables, by table, with its row label and path, column header, value as printed and in units, unit, currency, scale, period and period end, and a `url` that opens the document at the figure. A prospectus prints thousands of figures, so this is off by default. A filing whose tables have not been read yet returns an empty `tables` with a `tables_note`.

## Items Incorporated by Reference

Public companies routinely satisfy an item by pointing at another document rather than repeating it — a proxy statement, an exhibit. By default such an item returns the cross-reference as filed.

Pass `resolve=true` to expand items incorporated by reference to their full text. Without it, an item can come back short and technically correct, which reads like missing data.

## Key SEC Filing Item Fields

Each item carries its item code — `Item-1A`, `Item-7`, and so on — and its text. `include_exhibits=true` attaches exhibit metadata to the response.

An answer-less response is possible: a filing that could not be parsed into items returns a successful response with no items. That case is not charged a credit.

## SEC Filing Items Date Convention

`year` and `quarter` refer to the filing's **period of report** — the fiscal period the document covers — not to the date it was filed. A 10-K covering fiscal 2025 filed in October 2025 is `year=2025`.

## SEC Filing Items Sources

Item text is taken from the filing itself on SEC EDGAR and split along the form's own item structure. Text is not summarised or rewritten.

## Query SEC Filing Items

Use the filing items endpoint to split one filing.

<ParamField query="ticker" type="string">
  The issuer's ticker. Required for a 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K; for a prospectus, pass this, `cik` or `accession_number`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="cik" type="string">
  SEC CIK. Prospectus only, for a company with no ticker yet.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="filing_type" type="string" required>
  `10-K`, `10-Q`, `8-K`, or a prospectus: `S-1` (any registration statement or prospectus of the company), `F-1`, `S-11`, `424B4` or `DRS`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="year" type="integer">
  Calendar year of the filing's period of report. Required for a 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K; for a prospectus, optional — the year it was filed.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="quarter" type="integer">
  Quarter 1 to 4. Required for a 10-Q.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="accession_number" type="string">
  The filing's accession number. Required for an 8-K; for a prospectus, picks one filing.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="item" type="string[]">
  Specific item codes to return, repeatable — for example `Item-1A` and `Item-7`, or prospectus sections such as `risk_factors` and `use_of_proceeds` (`all` for the whole prospectus). Omit to return all items of a 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K, or a prospectus's summary and table of contents.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="text_format" type="string" default="body">
  Prospectus only: `sec-html-1` returns the stored table-preserving text, one table row per line.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_tables" type="boolean" default="false">
  Prospectus only: also return every figure of the prospectus's tables, by table.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include_exhibits" type="boolean" default="false">
  Attach exhibit metadata to the response.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="resolve" type="boolean" default="false">
  Expand items incorporated by reference to their full text.
</ParamField>

This route returns a bare wrapper key rather than the standard envelope, and uses the flat `{ error, message }` error body.
